Why do I need to tap my passkey?

Your passkey is the thing that makes an approval yours. When you tap it (fingerprint, face or PIN on your phone or laptop), your device signs a record of exactly what you saw on this screen — the invoice, the amount, the vendor and the time. Nobody can approve on your behalf with just your password or a copied browser session.

How often? Once per sitting for ordinary invoices: one tap opens a signing session that lasts about 15 minutes between clicks (an hour at most). Invoices at or above the step-up amount, and any invoice for a health-care professional, ask for a tap of their own.

If the passkey prompt does not appear, make sure you are using the device where you enrolled it, or enroll this one under My passkeys.
